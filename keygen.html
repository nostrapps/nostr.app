What is a Nostr Key Pair?

A Nostr key pair consists of a private key and a public key. The private key is like your password - keep it secret and secure. The public key is your identity on the Nostr network.

With your key pair, you can sign messages, create content, and interact with other Nostr users across the decentralized network.

Generate New Keys

Click the button below to generate a new random key pair. This will create a completely new Nostr identity.

⚠️ Important Security Warning

Your private key is the only way to access your Nostr account. Store it securely and never share it with anyone. If you lose it, you will lose access to your account permanently.
